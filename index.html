Our Process

What It's Like Working With Us

Most people calling us for the first time aren't thinking about POP designs or paint brands yet — they're thinking about whether this is going to be a headache. Fair enough. A lot of finishing jobs in Nigeria go wrong not because the work is bad, but because nobody ever sat down and told the client what was actually going to happen, in what order, and why.

So here's that, honestly.

It starts with us coming to look at the actual space — not quoting blind off a phone call. A room photographed badly, or described vaguely, can hide a lot: a wall that isn't as straight as it looks, a ceiling height that changes the lighting plan, a window shape that rules out certain curtain styles. Joseph or someone on the team walks the space, asks what you're picturing, and tells you plainly if something you want needs adjusting — better to hear that at the visit than after the paint is dry.

From there comes the quote, broken down by what it actually covers — materials, labour, the finish type — so you're not left guessing what you're paying for. Once that's agreed, the work itself follows a deliberate order: surfaces get prepared and screeded before anything decorative touches them, because a POP design or a feature panel only looks as good as the wall underneath it. Painting, panelling, curtains and blinds come after, in whatever sequence the job needs.

The part we take seriously, maybe more than people expect, is the handover. We don't consider a job finished because the last coat dried — we walk the space with you, point out the detailing ourselves, and deal with anything that isn't sitting right before we call it done.

None of this is complicated. It's just the difference between a team that disappears after collecting payment and one that's still answering your calls six months later.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to what clients ask us most. Don't see yours? Message us and we'll reply.

What services does Ajis Interior Creations offer?

We handle interior and exterior painting, wall screeding, stucco design, Tyrolean finish, Gravitex paint texture, POP ceiling designs, TV wall consoles, wallpaper, murals, carpet grass, curtain and window blind installation, and 3D and fluted wall panels. You can read about each one on our Services page.

Where do you work?

We're based in Choba, Port Harcourt, and take on jobs across Nigeria, including Lagos, Abuja, Ondo State and Osun State. If your location isn't listed, just ask — we'll let you know if we can take it on.

How do I get a quote?

Reach out through the contact page, WhatsApp, or phone with a short description of what you need. We'll arrange a free site visit or discuss it over a call, then give you a quote for the job.

How much will my project cost?

It depends on the size of the space, the condition of the walls or ceiling, and the finish you choose, so we don't quote fixed prices without seeing the job. Share photos or book a site visit and we'll give you a clear figure.

How long does a job take?

A single room repaint is much quicker than a full house finish with POP ceilings and panels. We'll give you a realistic timeline once we've seen the space and agreed the scope with you.

Do you do both interior and exterior work?

Yes. We paint and finish interior walls and ceilings, and we also do exterior work such as painting, stucco, Tyrolean finish and Gravitex texture on outside walls and fences.

Can I see examples of your work before I decide?

Of course. Browse our portfolio and the videos on this page, or follow us on TikTok (@interiorajis) and Instagram for more recent projects.

How long have you been doing this?

Our founder, Joseph Ajiodo, started out as a painter in 2005 and later studied interior design. You can read his story on the founder page.
